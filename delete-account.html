Delete your account

LiftRoutine by the LiftRoutine developer

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open LiftRoutine and sign in.
  2. Tap Me and scroll to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all its data are deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email liftroutine.app@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my LiftRoutine account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Your name, email address, account ID, profile (goal, weight, height), and all workouts, records, awards and statistics. Nothing is kept afterwards, except where the law requires us to keep a record (for example, subscription purchase records held by Google Play).

Guest users

If you never created an account, your data exists only on your phone. Uninstall the app or clear its storage to delete it.
