Become a tester

Help launch LiftRoutine on Google Play. You need an Android phone and the Gmail account you use on the Play Store. It takes 1 minute.

Step 1 · Join the testers group

Tap the button, sign in with your Play Store Gmail and press Join group.

Join the group

Step 2 · Become a tester

On your Android phone, tap the button, then Become a tester, then Download it on Google Play.

Become a tester

Step 3 · Keep it for 14 days

Please keep LiftRoutine installed for 14 days and open it a few times. Found a bug or have an idea? Email liftroutine.app@gmail.com.

“Item not found” on Google Play? Wait a few minutes after joining the group, and make sure you used the same Gmail as on your phone’s Play Store. iPhones can’t test.
