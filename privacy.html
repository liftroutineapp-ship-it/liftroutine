Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

LiftRoutine ("the app", "we") is an independent app. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. We keep it simple: we only use your information to run the app for you. We never sell it and we don't show ads.

1. Using the app without an account

You can use LiftRoutine as a guest. Everything you enter — your name, training plan, workouts, body weight, height and settings — is stored only on your phone. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

2. If you create an account

If you choose to create an account, we store the following with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) so your progress is backed up and available when you sign in:

  • Your name and email address, and an account ID
  • Your profile: goal, experience level, body weight and height (used for BMI and calorie estimates)
  • Your training data: plan, workouts, sets, records, awards and statistics
  • Basic details such as sign-up date, last sign-in, app version and platform

This data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Google acts as our service provider; see Firebase privacy information.

3. What we don't collect

No location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising ID or browsing data. No third-party analytics or advertising tools are used.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification data is sent to us.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties. When you share a workout card, it is sent only to the app you pick, by you.

6. Subscriptions (when available)

If LiftRoutine Pro is offered, purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We don't receive your card or payment details — only confirmation that a subscription is active.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep account data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app (Me › Account › Delete account) or by following these steps. Deletion is permanent and completed within 30 days at the latest (normally immediately).

8. Health information

LiftRoutine is a fitness log and planner, not a medical service. BMI and calorie numbers are estimates for general guidance only.

9. Children

LiftRoutine is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data. Most of this you can do in the app; for anything else email us. If you are in India, the EU/UK or elsewhere, you have the rights given by your local data-protection law.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app for important changes.
